================================================================================
EXPERIMENT 7: 2:1 Multiplexer and Transmission Gate Design
================================================================================

AIM:
To design and simulate a CMOS Transmission Gate (TG) and TG-based 2:1 Multiplexer 
in DSCH and Microwind, analyzing ON-resistance and switching performance.

TRANSMISSION GATE PRINCIPLE:
- Parallel combination of 1 NMOS and 1 PMOS transistor.
- Controlled by complementary control signals: S (gate of NMOS) and ~S (gate of PMOS).
- Pass characteristics:
  * NMOS passes a strong logic '0' (0V) and degraded logic '1' (VDD - Vtn).
  * PMOS passes a strong logic '1' (VDD) and degraded logic '0' (|Vtp|).
  * In parallel, the TG passes full-rail rail-to-rail signals (0V to VDD) without threshold drop.

2:1 MUX USING TRANSMISSION GATES:
- 2 Transmission Gates + 1 CMOS Inverter = 6 transistors total.
- Compared to conventional gate-level MUX (2 AND + 1 OR + 1 NOT = 12 transistors), 
  the TG implementation saves 50% silicon area and dynamic switching power.
