================================================================================
EXPERIMENT 6: CMOS Half Adder and Full Adder Mask Layout
================================================================================

AIM:
To design the transistor-level schematic and mask layout for a 1-bit CMOS Half Adder 
and Full Adder in DSCH and Microwind, verifying functional truth tables and carry delay.

KEY ARCHITECTURES:
1. CMOS Half Adder:
   - Sum = A ^ B (CMOS XOR cell, 8-10 transistors)
   - Carry = A & B (CMOS AND = NAND2 + Inverter, 6 transistors)
2. CMOS Full Adder (Mirror Adder / Static CMOS 28-transistor):
   - Pull-up and pull-down networks designed with dual complementary topologies.
   - Carry cell optimized for minimal carry-propagation delay t_carry.
   - Full layout incorporates VDD upper rail, VSS lower rail, and well polarization taps.
================================================================================
