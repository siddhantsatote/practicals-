================================================================================
EXPERIMENT 1: Design, Synthesis, and Analysis of 4-bit Ripple Carry Adder (ASIC Flow)
================================================================================

AIM:
To design a 4-bit Ripple Carry Adder (RCA) using Verilog and VHDL, perform functional 
simulation, ASIC-oriented synthesis, and analyze area, power, and timing, identifying 
the critical path and delay.

APPARATUS / TOOLS REQUIRED:
- Cadence Genus / Synopsys Design Compiler / OpenLane / Xilinx ISE
- DSCH 3.5 & Microwind 3.5 (Schematic and Mask Layout)
- Verilog Simulator (ModelSim / ISim / Icarus Verilog)

THEORY:
A Ripple Carry Adder consists of cascaded Full Adders. Each full adder computes:
  Sum  = A ^ B ^ Cin
  Cout = (A & B) | (B & Cin) | (A & Cin)

The carry output of each stage ripples to the next, making the total delay proportional 
to the bit-width N:
  Worst-case delay = N * t_carry

Why RCA is important in ASIC design:
- Simple structure & low cell area
- Predictable regular routing
- Baseline reference for high-speed adder architectures (Carry Lookahead, Carry Select)

SYNTHESIS STEPS (ASIC FLOW):
1. Read HDL source files (FULL_ADDER.v, ripple_carry_adder_4bit.v).
2. Set target standard cell library (e.g., 45nm / 28nm standard cells).
3. Apply timing and design constraints (clock frequency, input/output arrival times, load capacitance).
4. Run logic synthesis and technology mapping (Genus: 'syn_generic', 'syn_map', 'syn_opt').
5. Generate reports: Area report, Timing report (critical path), Power report.
6. Export synthesized Gate-level Netlist (.v) and SDC constraints for Place & Route (P&R).

OBSERVATIONS & CRITICAL PATH:
- Critical Path: From A[0]/B[0]/Cin through FA0 carry -> FA1 carry -> FA2 carry -> FA3 sum/cout.
- Area: Minimal (4 Full Adders, 8 LUTs / 36 equivalent gates).
- Timing: Linear propagation delay O(N).
