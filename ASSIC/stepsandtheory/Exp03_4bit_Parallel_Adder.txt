================================================================================
EXPERIMENT 3: Design, Synthesis and Simulation of 4-bit Parallel Adder
================================================================================

AIM:
To design, simulate, and synthesize a 4-bit parallel adder using either Behavioral, 
Dataflow, and Structural modeling styles in Verilog HDL, and to analyze power, area 
(LUTs/gates), critical timing, and delay targeting an ASIC design workflow.

THEORY & MODELING STYLES:
1. Behavioral Modeling:
   - High-level algorithmic description using arithmetic operators (+).
   - `always @(*) {Cout, Sum} = A + B + Cin;`
   - Optimized automatically by EDA synthesis tool (inferred carry lookahead or ripple).
2. Dataflow Modeling:
   - Continuous assignment statements using Boolean equations.
   - `assign {Cout, Sum} = A + B + Cin;`
3. Structural Modeling:
   - Explicit instantiation of standard library logic gates or Full Adder sub-modules.
   - Accurately mirrors physical gate-level ASIC implementation.

METRIC COMPARISON SUMMARY:
  Metric              Behavioral      Dataflow        Structural
  -------------------------------------------------------------------
  Area (LUTs/Gates)   Optimized       Optimized       Highest
  Power Consumption   Lowest          Medium          Highest
  Critical Path Delay Tool-dependent  Medium          High (Ripple Carry)
  Timing Accuracy     Low (Pre-syn)   Medium          High (Direct gate mapping)

CONCLUSION:
Structural modeling provides exact timing and power correlation for ASIC sign-off, 
while behavioral modeling gives the fastest design entry and synthesis optimization.
