================================================================================
EXPERIMENT 04: STEPPER MOTOR / L293D DRIVER CIRCUIT
================================================================================

Microcontroller: 8051
Driver IC: ULN2003A (Darlington Array) or L293D (Dual H-Bridge)
Motor: 5-Wire Unipolar / 4-Wire Bipolar Stepper Motor

Wiring:
  8051 Pin         Driver Pin          Motor Coil
  -------------------------------------------------------------
  P2.0 (Pin 21) -> IN1 (Pin 1)      -> Phase A (Coil 1)
  P2.1 (Pin 22) -> IN2 (Pin 2)      -> Phase B (Coil 2)
  P2.2 (Pin 23) -> IN3 (Pin 3)      -> Phase C (Coil 3)
  P2.3 (Pin 24) -> IN4 (Pin 4)      -> Phase D (Coil 4)

Power:
  Driver GND (Pin 8)  -> System GND
  Driver COM (Pin 9)  -> +12V Motor Power Supply
  Driver OUT1-OUT4    -> Connected to stepper motor winding leads
================================================================================
