================================================================================
EXPERIMENT 05: BUTTON AND LED INTERFACING
================================================================================

1. AIM:
   To interface push button switches and 8 LEDs to 8051 and program interactive
   lighting patterns with button debouncing.

2. THEORY:
   - Port 1 is connected to 8 LEDs. When a pin is driven LOW, LED turns ON (active-low mode).
   - Push button is connected to P3.2 with an external 10k pull-up resistor.
   - Switch Debounce: Mechanical switches vibrate for 10-20 ms upon contact.
     Software debouncing uses a 20 ms delay after detecting a press to ensure steady state.

3. VIVA QUESTIONS & ANSWERS:
   Q1: Why must an 8051 port pin be written with '1' before being used as an input?
   A1: Writing '1' turns off the internal pull-down FET, allowing the internal pull-up resistor
       to hold the pin high so external devices can pull it low.
