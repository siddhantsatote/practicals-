================================================================================
EXPERIMENT 07: 4x4 MATRIX KEYPAD INTERFACING
================================================================================

1. AIM:
   To interface a 4x4 matrix keypad to 8051 and decode the pressed key.

2. THEORY & SCANNING ALGORITHM:
   - 4 Rows (P1.0-P1.3) and 4 Columns (P1.4-P1.7) form a matrix of 16 keys.
   - Column scanning: Drive one column LOW at a time (0xEF, 0xDF, 0xBF, 0x7F).
   - Check which row input reads LOW.
   - The intersection of the active LOW column and LOW row uniquely identifies the pressed key.
   - Lookup table translates row and column coordinates into ASCII characters.

3. VIVA QUESTIONS & ANSWERS:
   Q1: How many pins are saved by using a matrix keypad?
   A1: 16 individual switches would require 16 pins. A 4x4 matrix requires only 8 pins (50% reduction).
