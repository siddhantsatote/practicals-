================================================================================
EXPERIMENT 03: INTERFACING 8051 WITH ADC0804 AND DAC0808
================================================================================

1. AIM:
   To interface 8051 microcontroller with ADC0804 to convert analog signals
   to 8-bit digital values, and DAC0808 to generate analog waveforms.

2. THEORY:
   - ADC0804 is an 8-bit Successive Approximation Register (SAR) ADC.
     Conversion steps:
     1. Pulse WR low then high to start conversion.
     2. Wait for INTR line to go LOW, signaling conversion complete.
     3. Drive RD low to read digital byte from Port 1.
   - DAC0808 is an 8-bit current-output multiplying DAC.
     Output current Iout is converted to voltage using an operational amplifier (LM741).
     Vout = Vref * (Digital_Byte / 256).

3. WAVEFORM GENERATION LOGIC:
   - Ramp / Sawtooth: Increment DAC byte in a loop from 0x00 to 0xFF.
   - Triangular: Increment from 0x00 to 0xFF, then decrement back to 0x00.
   - Square Wave: Alternate outputting 0x00 and 0xFF with equal time delays.

4. VIVA QUESTIONS & ANSWERS:
   Q1: What is the resolution of an 8-bit ADC with 5V reference?
   A1: Step size = Vref / 2^8 = 5V / 256 = 19.53 mV per LSB.
   Q2: What is the function of the INTR pin in ADC0804?
   A2: It goes active-low when conversion is complete (End of Conversion / EOC).
