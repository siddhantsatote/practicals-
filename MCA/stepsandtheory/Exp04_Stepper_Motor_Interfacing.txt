================================================================================
EXPERIMENT 04: STEPPER MOTOR INTERFACING WITH 8051
================================================================================

1. AIM:
   To interface a stepper motor with 8051 using ULN2003 / L293D driver and control
   its rotation direction (CW/CCW) and speed using timer-based delays.

2. THEORY:
   - Stepper motor rotates in discrete angular steps per input electrical pulse.
   - Standard 4-step sequence (Full-Step Mode):
     Step 1: 0x09 (Coil A & D active)
     Step 2: 0x0C (Coil A & B active)
     Step 3: 0x06 (Coil B & C active)
     Step 4: 0x03 (Coil C & D active)
   - Clockwise (CW): Step 1 -> Step 2 -> Step 3 -> Step 4 -> Repeat.
   - Counter-Clockwise (CCW): Step 4 -> Step 3 -> Step 2 -> Step 1 -> Repeat.
   - Speed control: Shorter delay between steps increases speed; longer delay decreases speed.
   - Driver requirement: 8051 output pins cannot supply the 500mA coil current; ULN2003
     Darlington array provides the required current drive.

3. VIVA QUESTIONS & ANSWERS:
   Q1: What is the step angle of a 200-step stepper motor?
   A1: Step Angle = 360 degrees / 200 = 1.8 degrees per step.
   Q2: Why is a flyback diode needed when driving stepper motor coils?
   A2: Coils are inductive loads; when switched off, they produce large back-EMF spikes that could
       damage transistors. ULN2003 contains built-in internal clamp diodes.
